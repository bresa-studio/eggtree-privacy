# Privacy Policy — Egg Tree

*Last updated: [04/11/2026]*

## 1. Data Controller

Egg Tree ("the Game") is developed and published by Bresa Studio. Privacy contact: [eggtree@bresastudio.com]. This policy explains what data is collected, for what purposes, and what rights you have.

## 2. Summary

The Game does not require registration. You may optionally link a Google account to your player so you can recover your progress and join events; if you do, we receive the email address of that Google account. Without a linked account your progress is stored locally on your device; with a linked account your stars and records are also stored in the cloud, so they follow you to another device, and they are deleted with your account. The Game uses analytics and crash reporting (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics), advertising (Google AdMob), and a global leaderboard, event and account service (Supabase). Your player name and competitive scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard and on event boards; your email address is never shown to other players. Versions 1.0.3 and earlier used GameAnalytics instead of the Firebase services.

## 3. Data the Game collects directly

- **Player name**: generated automatically and editable from the menu. It is shown next to your scores on the leaderboard and on event boards. It is the only information you enter that could be linked to you.
- **Google account (optional)**: if you link a Google account from the menu, we receive its email address through Google Sign-In. The email address is stored only in our authentication service. It is used to contact you about your account and in-game events and to answer your requests, and it is never shown to other players, never sent to analytics or crash reporting, and never sold. We do not receive your Google password.
- **Approximate country**: derived from the IP address your device connects from when reaching our servers (via our infrastructure provider's geolocation) or, when that is not available, from the region of your device's language settings. This is not precise geolocation and does not reveal your exact location — only a country-level approximation. It is shown as a flag next to your name and decides which events are available in your country.
- **Installation identifier**: a random identifier created on your device that associates your data across sessions and powers the leaderboard. It cannot identify you personally.
- **Scores and progress**: records, completed levels, and the current run. Stored locally and, if you link a Google account, also in the cloud (the stars of every level and your records, so they follow you to another device and are deleted with your account); competitive-mode scores are published on the leaderboard, and event scores are published on the board of the event.
- **Event runs**: when you play an event, we store the score of each run and a record of the moves of your best run (taps and their timing, with no audio, image or text), so we can verify the results of events.

## 4. Third parties that process data

- **Supabase**: anonymous identity, optional Google account linking, global leaderboard and events. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Linking is optional: without it no account with an email or password is created.
- **Google Sign-In**: only if you link a Google account. Google shows you its account picker and tells us which account you chose (its email address) and proves that it is yours. See Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Analytics for Firebase**: game analytics. It receives gameplay events (for example the start and end of a run, the score reached, a level completed, or a screen opened), the app version, the platform, an approximate country, and an app-instance identifier generated by the SDK. Because the Game includes advertising, it may also receive your device's advertising identifier. It does not receive your player name, your email address, your installation identifier, or any other personal data you enter. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics collection is off. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: crash reporting. When the Game stops unexpectedly it sends a report with the error trace, the device model, the operating system version, the app version, the time of the error, and an installation identifier generated by the SDK. It does not receive your player name or your email address. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, crash reporting is off. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Google AdMob**: advertising. It may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, and ad interaction data, per Google's [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent (through Google's UMP platform) before showing personalized ads; without consent, ads are shown without personalization.
- **GameAnalytics** (versions 1.0.3 and earlier only): game analytics (gameplay events, session duration, country, app version, platform, and an installation identifier generated by the SDK). It does not receive your player name or any other personal data you enter. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, event submission to GameAnalytics is off by default. Later versions do not include this service. See its [privacy policy](https://www.gameanalytics.com/trust/privacy-notice).

## 5. How we use data

To improve the Game and its difficulty, find and fix errors, maintain the leaderboard and rankings, run in-game events and verify their results, analyze usage, and show ads and measure their performance. **We do not sell personal data.**

## 6. Data we do NOT collect

Phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, exact location, financial data, or in-app purchases (the Game does not include them). We collect an email address only if you choose to link a Google account.

## 7. Consent and control

- **Ads**: you can withdraw consent or receive non-personalized ads from your device settings or Google's ([Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com)). You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier from your device settings.
- **Analytics, crash reports and leaderboard**: in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, analytics and crash reporting are off and no action is needed to disable them further. Outside those regions, the Game does not include an in-app switch to disable them.
- **Google account**: linking is optional and you can decide not to link one. You can delete your account at any time, as described in [Account deletion](#account-deletion).
- **Event reminders**: the Game can show local reminders before and after an event you joined closes. They are scheduled on your device and send no data; you can turn them off from your device's notification settings.
- **Deletion**: uninstalling the application removes the local data on your device but does not delete your server-side data; to delete it, use the steps in [Account deletion](#account-deletion).

## 8. Local storage

Progress, identity, and settings are stored in your device's internal storage and are only transmitted as described in this policy.

## 9. Retention

Your profile, records and event results are kept as long as the game identity exists or until you delete your account. Daily leaderboard entries are deleted 60 days after their day. Thirty days after an event closes, we delete every run of that event except each player's best run and its record of moves, which are kept with the event's final board. The email address of a linked Google account is kept until you delete your account. Analytics data is kept by Google Analytics for up to 14 months, and crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days. Data collected by versions 1.0.3 and earlier is kept per GameAnalytics' policy. Local data is kept until you uninstall the application or clear its data.

## 10. Children

The Game is directed at users aged 13 and older. It has received a content rating of PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / IARC 3+ (suitable for all ages). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; if you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us at [eggtree@bresastudio.com].

## 11. Changes

If we modify this policy, we will update this page and its last-updated date.

## 12. Account deletion {#account-deletion}

You can delete your account and the data associated with it in either of these ways:

- **In the game**: open the main menu, tap your name, tap the Google account row, tap **Delete account** and confirm. The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. The Game then starts over with a new anonymous player.
- **By email**: write to [eggtree@bresastudio.com] from the address of your linked Google account (or tell us your player name) and ask us to delete your account. We will do it and confirm within 30 days.

What is deleted: your player profile and name, your scores and your positions on leaderboards and event boards, the runs and records of moves of your event runs, any progress stored in the cloud, and the link to your Google account together with the email address we received from it. We do not keep a copy. Data that Google processes under its own policies (for example your Google account itself) is not affected. Local data on your device is removed when you uninstall the application or clear its data.
